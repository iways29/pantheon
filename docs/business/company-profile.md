# The Unreal Lab: company profile

A description of the studio, its audience, products and voice, taken from the
company brief (`the-unreal-lab.md`) for the agents' library. It describes; the
rules the agents follow live in their own instructions.

## What the studio is

The Unreal Lab is an AI venture studio. It began as an AI product lab. Its
longer-term path is a studio now, a portfolio next, and a venture fund later.

It works in four ways:

- **It builds** its own AI products, shipped and live: Mumba.ai and ASHVAA.
  The next product is unnamed.
- **It advises** companies on enterprise AI that has to work reliably:
  governance, evaluation and agent platforms.
- **It partners**: it is building relationships with product companies, with
  the aim of later deploying the right ones inside the enterprises its partners
  serve. This work is at an early stage and looks forward.
- **It backs** founders at the very beginning, before there is a deck or a
  company, with architecture, people, introductions, launch help and hours, for
  a small agreed stake. Capital comes later, as the portfolio earns it.

Founders apply with one email. No deck is required. Every application is read,
and when the model fits the studio answers within a week.

## The products

**Mumba.ai** is a branching AI canvas: "One Question. Every Direction." Any
reply can be branched into a parallel thread that keeps its own context.
Several threads run side by side, each on the model the user picks (Claude,
GPT or Gemini), and every branch sits on one canvas. Smart Merge combines the
branches a user selects into one answer. It is free during its beta with no
credit card. It is built in public, with a five-phase roadmap: proof of
concept and MVP shipped or live; growth (subscriptions, a refined UI, custom
models) planned for Q3 2026; then deep research, artifacts and MCP
connectors; then enterprise features. Its own product voice is crisp and
short: "Chat Is A Straight Line. Your thinking isn't."

**ASHVAA** is open-source codebase intelligence under the MIT licence. It
reads a GitHub repository and returns a dependency map, security findings,
dead code and an AI health score. It is early, with a plain technical README.

## Who it speaks to

1. Young founders who are too early for everyone else.
2. Backers: high-net-worth individuals and, later, limited partners.
3. Enterprise buyers and product companies, for advisory and partnerships.

The owner's aim for every piece is that it draws in both young founders and
backers.

## The idea behind the studio

The studio is the charioteer and the founder is Arjuna: "Every Arjuna needs a
Krishna." The second line is "We make the unreal real." A second story is
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and self-rule built one fort at a time: "Swarajya
was not declared. It was built, one fort at a time."

Recurring themes: the chariot, the field, the bow, forts built one at a time,
self-reliance, access and depth over fundraising stories, and real execution.
Lines from the site that show the tone: "You already hold the bow. You are
only doubting your right to draw it." "Access is the advantage no term sheet
lists." "Speed where the incumbents were slow."

## The voice

The studio sounds philosophical and practical, confident without hype.
Restraint is the brand: precise and unhurried. Its temperament:

- composed and in control, with calm authority;
- strategic and patient, talking about the position rather than the noise;
- few words, chosen well: short declarative sentences;
- loyal to its founders, who get the credit;
- a dry, understated edge, never jokes or swagger;
- confidence that comes from specifics and results;
- decisive: it takes a position and names the next step.

Good writing for the studio uses short sentences, one idea per paragraph,
concrete numbers and named things, and ends without a flourish. Emoji appear
sparingly. Everything outside the website is in English; mythology is told in
English.

## Channels

Reddit, Instagram, X, a weekly newsletter, and a blog post on the site every
two weeks or as needed. The blog post is the anchor; the other channels carry
versions of it. The owner posts by hand. Visuals are simple drawn graphics in
the site's look by default, and generated images for specific pieces.

The site's look: ink `#100c08`, paper `#f4efe4`, body `#eadfc4`, gold
`#e6c76a`; Libre Caslon Display italic and Archivo; square corners, no
shadows; bold restraint.
