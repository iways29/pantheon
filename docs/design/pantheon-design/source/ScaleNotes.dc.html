The brain is a lens

The disc is a window onto a space with no edge. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. The dial and the agent ring stay fixed; only the brain moves.

Activity outside the view shows as a chevron on the rim, pointing to where it happened. Threads to a neighbourhood outside the view end at the rim.
The view never moves on its own. Events don’t pull the lens around. “Follow this order” and tapping a rim chevron move it smoothly, and only when you ask.
Places stay put. Positions come from similarity and are only ever added to. A new fact lands beside its nearest neighbours, and a new topic grows at the edge of the space, so what you remember is where you left it.
Past 50,000 facts neighbourhoods group into regions, such as Market or Products, which adds one more zoom level above the ones shown here.

When space runs outAgents move closer together. Workers lose their labels; heads and anyone waiting keep them. Hover or focus shows the name.
Threads still workA thread to an agent in a closed department ends at its hub, so an order’s path stays visible.
